The Warrior's Peace

An Insider's Guide to Building Hope And a Positive New Future After Military Service

Many veterans struggle with life following military service. The Warrior's Peace provides a candid, firsthand exploration of the prolonged effects of exposure and the personal toll of dedicated service on normal human beings.

Written to help military veterans and their loved ones, this guide offers tools for recovery, sharing intense experiences, reflections, and conclusions to seek a better understanding of life's meaning after service. The goal is to provide new understanding and meaning to a complex lifestyle and patterns of thought that may seem foreign to those who live outside the military culture.

Behind the Words

Colonel Randal Schwallie

Colonel Randal Schwallie spent over thirty-two years in the US Army. A graduate of the United States Military Academy at West Point, his extensive career included deployments to Saudi Arabia and Iraq during the Gulf War (Operations Desert Shield and Desert Storm).

Throughout his time in uniform, he served in three separate functional branches, working as a Brigade and Task Force Commander, and completing three tours at the Pentagon. Following a career of dedicated service, he is now retired and resides in Utah.

The Warrior's Peace is his effort to provide inspiration, hope, and practical coping tools to fellow veterans navigating the complex transition back to civilian life, drawing deeply from both personal experience and the shared bonds of the military brotherhood.
